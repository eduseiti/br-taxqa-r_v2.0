How retrieval is scored across strategies that cut the text differently

S1 indexes whole articles; S2 also indexes caput, paragraphs, incisos, alíneas and items. A raw chunk rank is therefore not comparable between them. The evaluation (scripts/rag/ragpipe/evaluation.py, relevance.py) scores one ranked list of chunks per question three ways, and always matches the gold at the level of the gold, not at the level of the chunk. Reference note: ; plan: .

Answer to the key question: "if the gold is an article, how are S2 retrievals evaluated?"

Matching (rule G2)

An article-level gold item (document, art N) is hit by any chunk of article N: the whole article, one of its split parts, its caput, any §, inciso, alínea or item. In S3/S4 a grouping chunk (chapter, section…) hits every article whose interval it contains. So an S2 inciso of art. 8 counts as "art. 8 retrieved".

MRR and nDCG@10 (unit view)

The chunk ranking is first projected to distinct articles: walk the ranking, and each article gets the position at which any of its chunks first appears; later chunks of an already-seen article are ignored. MRR = 1 / position of the first gold article in that list; nDCG@10 uses binary gain over the first 10 distinct articles. The position is therefore "the n-th distinct article", whatever the chunk size, and it is the position of any part of the article, not of the whole-article chunk.

Recall within the 4k budget (headline)

The context the generator sees is built from the ranking (score order, containment de-duplication with merge, overflow skip) until 4,000 Qwen3 tokens. A gold article counts as recalled when any of its chunks is in that context. Position inside the context does not matter. This is the only view that accounts for chunk length: short S2 chunks let more distinct articles fit.

Consequences to keep in mind. (1) At article level, S2 gets credit for an article when only one inciso of it is in the context; the generator then sees that inciso, not the whole article. The provision level is the stricter test. (2) At provision level there is no MRR/nDCG: S1 can only return the whole article containing a gold inciso, so there is no clean first position. (3) Article-level metrics are averaged over the 553 questions with at least one article gold; document level over 596; provision level over 558. The 119 questions without citations are absent from all retrieval metrics (but present in the answer scores).

How answers are scored with nuggets

Each official answer was broken into nuggets, short facts a correct answer must state, labelled vital or okay (frozen set nuggets/gold/v0-auto/). A judge (Gemini 3.8 Flash, prompt assign_v2) reads a system answer and labels every nugget support, partial_support, not_support or contradicts. Metrics for this one question

From one question to a run score

  • Per question first, then the mean. Every metric is computed per question and the run value is the plain mean over questions (macro average). A question with 115 nuggets weighs as much as one with 2.
  • Questions without vital nuggets have no V_strict / V (NaN) and drop out of those means. That is why paired comparisons say n = 714 for V_strict and 715 for A_strict.
  • Abstentions and errors are not sent to the judge: every nugget is not_support (source = abstained / error). An abstaining generator is therefore penalised on recall but not on contradictions.
  • Oracle runs cover only the 596 questions with a gold provision in the corpus; compare them with S1/S2 only through the paired comparisons (which use the common questions, n = 595).
  • Context support uses the same nuggets and the same labels, but the judge reads the context instead of the answer (prompt assign_ctx_v1). It measures what the context makes available, including passages the P&R did not cite.
  • Paired tests: per-question differences, 95 % paired bootstrap CI, sign-flip permutation p, and a chapter-cluster bootstrap (the 48 P&R chapters resampled as clusters).
Retrieval metrics score citations (did the context hold the provisions the P&R cites?). Nugget metrics score content (does the answer, or the context, state the facts of the official answer?). They can disagree: Gemini's contexts hold almost the same cited articles as jua's (+0.01 recall) but more of the answer's facts (+0.04–0.05 context V_strict).
