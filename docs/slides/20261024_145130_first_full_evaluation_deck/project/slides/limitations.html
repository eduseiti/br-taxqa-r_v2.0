Construct · LLM-made measurement

No human calibration yet of nuggets or judge; synthetic corruptions are cleaner than real errors.

Internal · one retrieval setting

One embedding model, dense only, answers at 4k only; Terra on S1 / S2 only (trimmed grid).

External · one domain, one year

Income tax 2024, one authority’s Q&A, Portuguese. Corpus = the cited law, not all IRPF law.

Contamination

GPT-5.6 may have seen the public Q&A. It inflates closed book, not the paired S1 / S2 contrast.

Ceiling audit is exploratory

57 questions, wide CIs; one question holds 12 of 60 cases; legal calls need a tax-law expert.

Small effect, few significant

Two of four generators on the primary set; no multiplicity correction for secondary contrasts.

The limitations a reviewer will raise, grouped by validity type. The biggest is that the whole measurement is model-made: we have strong automatic evidence, but no human calibration sample yet; the 72 disagreements between the two judges are the natural starting point. Second, a single dense retriever and a single answer budget; a different embedding model or a reranker could change the size of the S2 effect. Third, generalisation: one domain and one year. Fourth, contamination: it matters for the closed-book interpretation, not for the paired comparison between strategies, which uses the same model and questions. Fifth, the ceiling audit is exploratory and small. Finally, the effect is small and only two of four generators are significant on the primary set; we present it as a modest, robust effect, not a large one.
