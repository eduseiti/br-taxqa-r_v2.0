Results · 715 questions · S1 contexts give the same picture (8–14 % contradictions)

17

The controls tell us what retrieval does here. Closed-book answers contradict at least one gold fact in 27 to 38 percent of questions; with retrieval it drops to 8 to 14 percent, for every generator. Recall improves a lot for the Maritaca models and moderately for Luna, but not for Terra, whose closed-book score is as high as with retrieval. Terra’s knowledge cutoff is after the Q&A was published, so it may have seen it; this is a contamination caveat for closed-book numbers only, since the RAG answers are paired against each other with the same model. For an applied tax assistant the contradiction drop is arguably the most important effect.
