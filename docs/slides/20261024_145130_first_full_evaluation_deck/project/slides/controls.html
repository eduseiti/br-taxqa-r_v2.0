Generators come in same-release size pairs from two vendors: Maritaca’s Brazilian-Portuguese models and OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 pair. The pairing exists to test H6, the size by strategy interaction. The three controls frame the strategies: closed book shows how much the model knows without retrieval (and the GPT models have a cutoff after the Q&A was published, so contamination is plausible); the oracle uses the cited articles as context and is the best a retriever could do with this corpus at 4k; the wrong-context control uses real legal text of an unrelated question and should score zero if answers come from the context. The grid was trimmed to fit a fixed budget: Terra only runs the main comparison.
