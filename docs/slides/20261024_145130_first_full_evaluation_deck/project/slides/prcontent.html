25

For the 60 unsupported vital nuggets we searched the whole corpus, lexically and densely, outside the cited articles, and had a strong model classify the cause with all the evidence; every classification was then re-checked against that evidence and 17 were changed. About half of the vital facts are fully stated by the cited law and a fifth in part. A small share was our own oracle construction. About one in fourteen is stated in legislation the answer does not cite. And about one in six is not in any legal text: it is the tax authority’s application of the law to the case, software instructions, or an administrative position. Q350 is a good example: the law says parents; the authority infers parents-in-law via joint filing. This is a finding about the dataset itself and bounds every RAG system on it. Caveats: small sample, one question carries 12 of the 60, and the legal calls need a tax-law reader.
