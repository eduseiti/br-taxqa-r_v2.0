Data and pipeline · chunk texts abbreviated (…); prefixes are the document title and ementa

6

A concrete example of the unit choice. In S1 the whole article 35 is one chunk of about 720 tokens: all seven incisos and five paragraphs. In S2 the article chunk is kept and, in addition, every caput, paragraph, inciso, alínea and item becomes its own chunk, carrying the text of its ancestors (here the caput’s opening) but not its siblings. Every chunk is prefixed with the document title and its ementa, the official summary, which is the “core chunks plus metadata” setting Fernandes et al. recommend. The S2 chunk is about one fifth of the size and states exactly the rule the question needs.
