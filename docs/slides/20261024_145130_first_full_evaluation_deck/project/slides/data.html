Receita Federal’s Perguntas e Respostas IRPF 2024: the tax authority’s own Q&A for the 2024 personal income-tax return. Answers are authoritative, in Portuguese, and cite their legal basis.

Corpus

Every cited act, fetched and parsed: statutes (laws, decrees, normative instructions, with annexes) and non-statutory texts (rulings, opinions). Revoked acts are excluded (the RIR/1999).

Gold levels

Document (596 questions), article (553), provision down to alínea (558). 119 questions cite nothing resolvable; they stay in the answer evaluation.

Data and pipeline · v2.0 extends BR-TaxQA-R (HF: unicamp-dl/BR-TaxQA-R) with a parsed legal corpus

5

BR-TaxQA-R v1 already had the questions and the references as text. Version 2.0 fetches every referenced act and parses it to LexML, the Brazilian XML standard for legislation, so each reference can be resolved down to the article or provision. The gold answers come from the tax authority, not from annotators, which makes them authoritative but also means they sometimes go beyond what the cited texts state; we return to that in the robustness part. The RIR/1999 was excluded after hand review of contexts: it is revoked, was cited once historically, and crowded out its replacement, the RIR/2018. The run with it is kept as an ablation.
