Results · dense retrieval, containment de-duplication; paired differences from the retrieval evaluation

13

H1 holds clearly. At equal token budget, the S2 context contains about ten points more of the cited articles at every budget from 1k to 16k; at provision level the gain is four to six points. We had assumed strategies would converge at large budgets; they do not. S3 only differs from S2 at 16k, which is expected because its grouping chunks are large. The caput variants retrieve slightly worse at article level, but they were designed for the generator, so only answer scores can judge them. So the precondition for H2 is met: S2 gives the generator more of the right provisions.
