Results · context support with prompt assign_ctx_v1; oracle = cited articles cut to 4k tokens (80 % of gold articles fit)

To understand the small effect we scored the contexts themselves: the judge checks each gold nugget against the context. Two limits appear. First, even the oracle context, the cited articles, fully supports fewer than half the vital nuggets at 4k; S2’s retrieval gain translates into very little extra content. Second, generators state only about half to two thirds of what their context supports. Leakage is low, so the RAG answers really do come from the context. And answer quality tracks context quality closely. The first limit raised an obvious question from a reviewer’s point of view: is that 48 percent an artefact of our oracle, our corpus or the context judge? The robustness section tests exactly that.
