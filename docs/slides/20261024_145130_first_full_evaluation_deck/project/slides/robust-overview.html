This slide is the map of the robustness section. The measurement chain has three LLM-made parts, the nuggets, the vital labels and the judge, plus the statistical unit. We attacked each one separately: cluster-aware statistics, nugget subsets, synthetic damage to the nugget set, two alternative nugget creators from other model families, and a second judge on real answers. The last row is different: it is not about S1 versus S2 but about the low ceiling, and it was added after we saw the context-support result, so it is exploratory.
