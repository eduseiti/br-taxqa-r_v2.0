Summary in one table. The precondition and the main hypothesis are supported: S2 retrieves better and answers better, robustly but modestly. None of the mechanism hypotheses we recorded in advance is supported: grouping chunks, the caput sentence, period and condition effects, and the size interaction all come out null at 4k. The mechanism we did find is different from the one we expected: S2 helps mainly by giving the generator smaller, focused units of the same facts. And the reason the gain is small is mostly in the data: the official answers state things that their cited provisions do not, which no segmentation can retrieve.
