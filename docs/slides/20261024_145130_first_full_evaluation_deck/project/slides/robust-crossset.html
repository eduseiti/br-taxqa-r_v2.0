Two threats specific to LLM-made evaluation. First, the nugget creator: a judge could favour answers phrased like the nuggets, or the nuggets could carry one model’s view of the answer. We rescored S1 and S2 for two generators with nugget sets written independently by Gemini 3.1 Pro and by Qwen3.8-Max. The difference keeps its sign and is significant under both; Terra becomes significant; the ranking of runs is identical. Second, the judge: Gemini 3.1 Pro relabelled 280 real answers; agreement with Flash is kappa 0.90.
