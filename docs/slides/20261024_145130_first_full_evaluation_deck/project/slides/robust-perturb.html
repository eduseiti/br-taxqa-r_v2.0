Here we damage the nugget set on purpose: delete a random share of nuggets, or flip vital and okay labels, a thousand times each, and count how often the S1 to S2 difference stays significant. The evaluation plan set the bar at 15 to 20 percent damage before any scoring. Both significant generators clear it; the point where fewer than half the replicates stay significant is around 40 percent deletion for Luna and beyond 50 percent for Sabiá-4. The direction of the effect never flips. For scale, the auditors estimated the real nugget error rate at about 1.3 percent before repair. Terra’s result is fragile under this test, consistent with its borderline p-value.
