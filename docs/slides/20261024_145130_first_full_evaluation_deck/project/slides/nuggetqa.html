11

Because the outcome variable is produced by LLMs, we validated both the nuggets and the judge before using them, against thresholds fixed in advance. Nugget agreement across three creator families is high. The model-based error estimate, nuggets flagged by two independent auditors, is 1.3 percent before repair. The auditors themselves were tested with planted errors. The judge was selected with known-answer tests: gold answers corrupted in controlled ways, including the most dangerous error in tax answers, a value from another year’s table. One gate was not met: three enumerated items in three questions remain uncovered after repair; they are listed, two are headings. Reviewers should note that synthetic corruptions are cleaner than real generator errors; the real-answer check comes in the robustness part.
