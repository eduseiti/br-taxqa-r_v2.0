This is the main result. The paired difference S2 minus S1 is positive for all four generators. For Luna and Sabiá-4 the interval excludes zero, with permutation p below 0.01, and the cluster bootstrap over chapters gives the same conclusion. For Terra the point estimate is positive but the interval touches zero under the primary nugget set; it becomes significant under both alternative nugget sets. Sabiazinho-4, the smallest model, shows essentially no gain. In relative terms, about ten percent for the two significant generators. So H2 is supported, with a small effect. The rest of the talk asks why it is small and whether it is real.
