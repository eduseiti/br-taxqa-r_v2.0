All five strategies are filters over the same chunk table, so the embedding model, the prefix and the index are identical; the only manipulated variable is which units are indexed. S2 keeps the article chunks, so it is a superset of S1. Paragraph chunks in S2 carry the article label but not the caput; that was a deliberate decision, and S2c and S4 test the alternative of adding the caput’s opening sentence. A full-caput variant, S5, is deferred. Non-statutory documents (rulings, opinions) are segmented by their own blocks and paragraphs in all strategies.
